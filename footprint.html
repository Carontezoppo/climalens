Air Pollution & Light Pollution Global · CAMS NRT / NASA Black Marble

Two complementary views of the human footprint on the planet. Day mode shows Aerosol Optical Depth at 550 nm — the fraction of sunlight scattered and absorbed by particulates and haze in the atmosphere, a direct proxy for air pollution — updated in near-real-time from Copernicus CAMS. Night mode switches to NASA Black Marble, annual composites of artificial light emission from 2012 to 2024.
